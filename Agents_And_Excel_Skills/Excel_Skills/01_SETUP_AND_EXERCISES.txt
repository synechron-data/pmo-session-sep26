PMO EXCEL SKILLS: SETUP AND EXERCISES

Open a working copy of PMO_Project_Tracker.xlsx. The workbook contains fictional Milestones, Actions and RAID data as of 16 October 2026.

INSTALLATION
In Excel Copilot, open Settings (...) > Manage plugins & skills > Custom skills. Create the OneDrive skills folder if needed, then open it. Copy the pmo-weekly-status and pmo-action-followup folders into it, keeping each SKILL.md inside its matching folder. Refresh the custom-skills list. Invoke a skill from Choose plugins and skills or with its @name. Office display language must be English. Availability depends on your tenant. If custom skills are absent, use each file's instruction body as a practice prompt; this does not install a skill.

EXERCISE 1
@pmo-weekly-status Create the weekly PMO status report using the as-of date in the workbook. Include achievements, overdue milestones, due-today work, upcoming milestones, open RAID entries and data-quality questions. Preserve source sheets.

EXERCISE 2
@pmo-action-followup Review the action log and prepare owner follow-up drafts. Separate overdue, due-today, upcoming and unknown-date actions. Flag unassigned actions. Do not send messages.

EXERCISE 3
On your working copy, mark A01 Completed after adding closure evidence to its Comments cell. Rerun the action skill and check that the item leaves the open-action workload. Do not treat this exercise edit as a real completion.

EXERCISE 4
On your working copy, change all three as-of dates to 19 October 2026. Rerun both skills and check which due-today and overdue lists change. Verify using the original due dates and status fields. Restore the original dates before comparing with the agent exercise.

SUBMISSION
Save your working workbook with generated outputs and one follow-up draft. Record any unsupported claims or incorrect classifications you corrected.
